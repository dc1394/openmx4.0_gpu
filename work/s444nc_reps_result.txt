Findings
--------
1. The memory reduction ENABLED the 3-node GPU point: the old binary OOMed
   there, the new one completes 25/25 SCF at 95-104 GiB host peak, and it is
   the cost/performance sweet spot (265 s; 4 or 6 nodes buy only 4-5% more).
2. 2-node GPU remains out of reach: all three runs hit the host OOM killer
   at 119/124 GiB.  The GPU floor for this case is 3 nodes.
3. GPU Diagonalization is flat at ~190-192 s from 3 to 6 nodes -- the
   13312-dim complex solve saturates the per-node H100s already at 3 nodes;
   the small Total gains come from Set_Hamiltonian etc. (15.3 -> 11.9 s).
4. The CPU path keeps scaling (1212 -> 736 s at 6 nodes) but never comes
   close: even 6 CPU nodes lose 2.9x to 3 GPU nodes' 265 s.
5. 4-node GPU improved from the old binary's 266.9 s to 254.8+-2.3 s (~4.5%),
   consistent with the removed per-call staging transfers.
6. Utot is bit-identical within every (path, node count) triple and agrees
   to ~12 digits across paths and node counts.
