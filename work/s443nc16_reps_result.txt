internally inconsistent and made a strong-scaling curve for gpusolver2

Verification
------------
Utot is bit-identical across the three reps of every (node count, config)
point -- 12 of 12 -- so each triple is a repetition of one deterministic
calculation, not three different ones.  |F|max agrees across all three
solver paths to 3.3e-10 Hartree/Bohr or better at every node count, against
a value of 1.09e-5, i.e. five orders of magnitude below the quantity itself.

Host memory (node 0 peak, of 124 GiB) leaves headroom everywhere at this
rank count: gpusolver 40-87, elpa2 31-74, gpusolver2 50-116.  The 1-node
gpusolver2 figure (115-116) is the one point still close to the ceiling --
that is the measurement that forced 16 ranks/node in the first place.
