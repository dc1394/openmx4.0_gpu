VERDICT: GEMMul8 ON is genuinely faster on cghelgeo: off costs +4.7 % Total
(+20.7 s) and +6.8 % Diagonalization (+23.4 s).  The previously quoted "+19 %
if off" came from the single bnode013 sample (527.8 s) and is retracted as a
slow-node artifact.  Set_Hamiltonian is ~2.9 s FASTER with gemmul8 off
(20.91 vs 23.80): with the workspace-size registration at 0, the resident
cache admits with a larger budget -- but this small gain is swamped by the
slower FP64 gemms in the solver.

--- peak host memory, node 0 (GiB) ---
on : 84-86/124 (v4 series)      off: 83-84/124
No memory penalty either way; device-side gemmul8 workspaces are per-call
cached but capped, and with off the workspace registration drops to zero.

Conclusion: keep scf.gemmul8.enable at its default (on) for cghelgeo-class
collinear Band workloads; the INT8 backend is worth ~5 % total.  (Contrast:
on the memory-tight sidia444_nc 2n case, off won by ~15 %, and on sidia443_nc
on/off were indistinguishable at every node count -- the knob is workload-
dependent and worth A/B-ing per system.)
