Findings
--------
1. 1-node GPU still hits the host OOM killer (all three runs, killed during
   SCF 1 at a 119/124 GiB peak).  The memory-reduced binary gets further than
   the old one (which died in setup), but 48 GPU ranks on one 124 GiB node do
   not fit this case.  1-node CPU completes at a 109-110 GiB peak.
2. Scaling saturates at 2 nodes on the GPU path: Diagonalization stays at
   ~29-30 s from 2 to 4 nodes (the 6656-dim real-symmetric solve is already
   GPU-bound); the Total gain 80.4 -> 73.7 s comes from the distributed
   phases (Set_Hamiltonian etc.).  The CPU path stops improving at 3 nodes,
   and its Diagonalization gets WORSE at 4 nodes (73.5 -> 82.3 s, ELPA2
   communication).
3. Best cost/performance: GPU 2 nodes (80.4 s, 2.16x over CPU).  Going to
   4 nodes buys only 9% more for twice the nodes.
4. Utot is bit-identical within every (path, node count) triple; across
   paths/node counts the values agree to ~12 digits as expected.
5. Consistency with the pre-fix single samples (GPU 2n 77.9 / 3n 74.6 /
   4n 74.0; CPU 1n 264.4 / 2n 171.0 / 3n 139.0 / 4n 143.7): all within
   ~2 sigma of the new means -- the host-memory reduction did not cost speed.
