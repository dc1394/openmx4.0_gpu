plateaued at ~0.83 TFLOPS, a 1:64 FP64:FP32 part).  GEMMul8 reaches
5.8 -> 52 TFLOPS with size.  s_GEMM (cuBLAS/G8, median-vs-median):
  D (real):    1024: 0.21  2808: 0.66  3900: 0.70  5616: 0.91  6656: 1.05  7800: 1.09

Crossover to G8-faster: Z at n ~= 3300, D at n ~= 6656.  Campaign shapes:
bcol216_back 1.04, bcol300 1.16-1.25, bnc216_back(5616) 1.28, ccol216 0.66,
ccol512 1.04, ccol360 0.64-0.79, cnc300_zback1(7800) 1.55.  Same accuracy as

contrast -- s collapses from ~20 to ~1 while f stays structural -- is the

Reading (the H100 sentences the referees will want):
- WHY the H100 campaign shows G8 ~= parity everywhere (Sm 0.98-1.02): a
  DOUBLE collapse.  s_eff is 0.64-1.19 (native FP64 tensor cores remove the
  emulation's advantage), AND f_diag is only 0.008-0.119 (the same cuBLAS
  speed makes the GEMM share of the diag bucket small -- syevdx dominates).
  S_pred_diag lands at 0.995-1.019 against S_meas_diag 0.978-1.018; at
  these effect sizes the residuals (<= 3%) are within the rep-to-rep SDs of
  the walls themselves.  The model's content on the H100 is explanatory:
  it predicts, correctly, that nothing moves.
- bnc216: the 16 GB fallback story disappears on 80 GB (z_fallbacks 0,
  back-transform runs G8 at s = 1.28) -- but s_eff 1.08 means recovering
  the G8 path is worth ~0.6% of diag, not the 5080's hypothetical 1.83x.
  Memory headroom restores the path; native FP64 strength removes the
  incentive.  One sentence for the paper: GEMMul8 pays where FP64 is weak
  and fits where memory is large -- the two GPUs each lack one condition.
- bcol300 total: S_meas_tot 0.870 vs S_pred_tot 1.012 is NOT a GEMM-model
  failure -- the g leg's Set_Hamiltonian rises 5.95 -> 16.56 s while diag
  closes at S 1.017 as predicted.  This is the documented Set_Ham
  device-residency interference (GEMMul8 workspaces evict SCF-invariant
  tables; see the device-headroom analysis), i.e. the h term of plan 14.3
  living OUTSIDE the GEMM sites.  The sibs scaling series reproduces it
  independently (0.889 +- for the same config).  Paper: quote bcol300@H100
  as the measured cost of the workspace-vs-residency conflict, not as a
  GEMMul8 kernel deficit.
- Wall provenance: siprod n=5 np48 means; the sibs series' n=7 rep set
  puts bcol300 S_meas_diag at 1.039 (g-leg CV 4.2% was flagged in siprod)
  -- both inside the same band as Sp_diag 1.019; the closure statement is
  rep-set-independent.

=== BANDPROF cross-check (prof_h100/, bcol300 o/g, 5 SCF, np48, MPS on) ===
Owner ranks (8), steady iterations 2-4: the gemmF+syevdx+gemmB block reads
o: 0.000 + 2.37 + 0.000 = 2.37 s/it   (async-attribution caveat as on the
                                       5080: cuBLAS Zgemm is asynchronous,
                                       its time is absorbed into syevdx)
g: 0.18  + 2.06 + 0.08  = 2.32 s/it
i.e. the in-app counterpart of s_eff ~= 1: replacing the GEMMs changes the
bucket by -2% on the H100, versus the 5080's 6.8 -> 1.3 s/it collapse.
syevdx itself (~2.0-2.1 s/it) dominates the H100 diag bucket, consistent
with f_diag ~= 0.12.  In-app per-owner GEMM walls under 8-way MPS
concurrency are again stretched (~0.26 s/it observed vs ~0.04 s isolated
x counts), which is why the model uses isolated microbench times x manifest
counts, exactly as documented for the 5080.
