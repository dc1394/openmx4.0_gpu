Single-GEMM microbenchmark + lightweight Amdahl assembly, RTX 5080
(2026-08-27) -- closes the "f and s_GEMM are not measured" gap flagged for
the paper: the campaign zip had no GEMM-level logs, so the speedup ratios
were descriptive only.  This directory adds the two missing artifacts:

  gemm_micro_5080.csv  (M2)  per-shape cuBLAS-FP64 vs GEMMul8 times
  amdahl_5080.csv      (M3)  per-system f, s_eff, S_pred vs S_meas

=== method ===
bench_gemm.cu replicates source/gemmul8_bridge.cu exactly: gemmul8::gemm
<double|cuDoubleComplex, Backend::INT8>, num_moduli 15, fastmode false
(the manifest-recorded production config), alpha=1 beta=0, natural leading
dims, one preallocated device workspace of gemmul8::workSize bytes; cuBLAS
side = plain cublasDgemm/Zgemm.  7 reps interleaved ABAB (equal thermal
footing), median; idle GPU, no MPS (single process), campaign library
third_party/GEMMul8/lib/libgemmul8.a (v3.2.0, 833e5761), nvcc 26.5 sm_120.
Shapes are the exact dense-solver calls, reconstructed from the run
manifests (gemmul8.d_calls/z_calls) + the call sites, and the counts close
exactly (shapes_campaign.txt header documents the per-solve structure):
  band col     3 Z/solve: NN,CN,TT all (n,n,n)            -> 600 Z/run = 8x25x3
  band NC      7 Z/solve: 3 blocks x {NN,CN} at (n2/2)^3
               + back TT (n2,n2,n2)                       -> 1400 Z/run
  cluster col  3 D/solve: NN,TN (n,n,n); back TT (MaxN,n,n),
               SCF1 MaxN=n                                -> 75 D/run
  cluster NC   12 D/solve at (n2/2)^3 + 1 Z back TT
               (MaxN,n2,n2), SCF1 MaxN=n2                 -> 300 D + 25 Z/run
  MaxN: ccol216 832 (=TZ/2+400), ccol512 1689 (=1024+0.1n), cnc216 1987
  (=864+0.2*n2); band back-transforms are full-n (MaxN affects syevdx only).

=== M2 highlights (gemm_micro_5080.csv, 42 shapes) ===
cuBLAS FP64 plateaus at ~820-836 GFLOPS on every shape >= 2048 (the RTX
5080's FP64 ceiling, 1:64 of FP32); GEMMul8 reaches 15.3-21.2 TFLOPS.
s_GEMM (cuBLAS/G8, median-vs-median) grows with size:

  Z (complex): 1024: 10.3x  2048: 15.3x  2808: 18.6x  4992: 23.7x  6656: 25.4x
Campaign shapes: bcol216 18.6-19.2x, bcol384 23.7-24.6x, bnc128 11.6x(fwd)/
17.5x(back), bnc216 18.6-19.1x(fwd)/24.0x(back 5616), ccol216 8.3-13.5x,
ccol512 15.3-18.8x, cnc216 13.1-13.4x(D)/19.7-23.9x(Z back).
Max |C_G8 - C_FP64| / max|C_FP64| <= 1.4e-14 on every shape (moduli-15
emulation is FP64-equivalent at the single-GEMM level too).

=== M3: lightweight Amdahl model (plan sec. 14.3), per-system-best np ===

(193 of 200 back-transform 5616^3 ZGEMMs fell back to cuBLAS, 7 ran G8).

Agreement: 5 of 7 systems within 1.5% on the diag speedup (bcol216 2.083
pred vs 2.096 meas; bnc216 1.255 vs 1.251 with the fallback mix; ccol216,
ccol512, cnc216 all <=1.4%); bcol384 +8% and bnc128 +5% over-predictions =
the h term of plan 14.3 (in-app integration overhead; largest matrices /
most calls).  Equivalent per-iteration statement: the modelled diag saving
(T_gemm_cu - T_gemm_g8)/25 matches the measured (diag_o - diag_g)/25 to
-0.6% (bcol216: 4.90 vs 4.92 s/it), +1.1% (bnc216), +1.0% (ccol216),
-0.7% (ccol512), +3.5% (cnc216), +6.7/+7.4% (bcol384/bnc128).

This answers the expected referee question quantitatively:
- WHY bcol ~2.1x diag: f_diag = 0.55-0.59 with s_GEMM ~19-24 -> the Amdahl
  ceiling 1/(1-f) is 2.2-2.4 and the campaign sits essentially on it.
- WHY bnc216 only 1.25x: SAME f (0.475) but s_eff collapses 21.5 -> 1.75
  because the dominant 5616^3 back-transform (1.70 s/call cuBLAS, 71 ms
  G8) ran on cuBLAS in 193 of 200 calls (16 GB reserve-policy fallback);
  the zero-fallback hypothetical recovers S_pred 1.83, consistent with
  bnc128's measured 1.67 and a direct prediction for the 80 GB H100.
- WHY ccol216 total barely moves (1.03x): f_total = 0.026 -- at np18 the
  diag bucket is 11% of total and cuSOLVER syevdx dominates it.

=== BANDPROF cross-check (prof/, bcol216 o/g, 5 SCF, np18, MPS on) ===
g leg, owner ranks: gemmF+syevdx+gemmB collapses 6.8 -> 1.3 s/it vs the o
leg -- the in-app counterpart of the GEMM replacement.  Caveat discovered:
in the o leg the per-call gemmF/gemmB buckets read 0.000 because
cublasZgemm is asynchronous and wait_cudafunc() only retries on the return
status (no device sync), so the cuBLAS GEMM execution is absorbed into the
next synchronizing bucket (syevdx's blocking memcpy).  gemmul8::gemm
synchronizes internally (it returns its stage timings), so the g leg's
buckets are real.  Per-call in-app GEMM walls under 8-way MPS concurrency
are stretched ~3x vs isolated, which is why the model uses isolated
microbench times x manifest counts (aggregate GPU throughput) rather than
in-app dtime -- the per-iteration closure above justifies this.
