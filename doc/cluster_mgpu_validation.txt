Single-node cluster multi-GPU validation (2026-10-14)
===================================================

Environment and baseline
------------------------
A100-SXM4-40GB, direct NVLink, CUDA 12.5, NVHPC 24.7, GCC 12.2 and
Open MPI 4.1.6 on ISSP Kugui. MAGMA 2.10.0 uses LP64 BLAS/LAPACK and
one-stage dsyevdx_m / zheevdx_m. CPU libraries use one thread in the
numerical comparisons. Local GPU allocations and MPS are required by the
site; allocation and MPS setup are outside the portable test runners.

The pre-change baseline was rebuilt from a clean archive of commit
475566134142d6b5653efe769f360dd0152c9618 with Makefile.kugui. This includes
the current numerical-correctness fixes and A100/GeForce policies.

  57fc427e63b96ab806f7f40f04d2107d8ab0b89c9323b60f8cb5e704bd9f7b5c
Candidate binaries are snapshots of the multi-GPU working tree, not a new
commit. Each application run records its binary and input hashes.

The final WITH_MAGMA=1 dependency/application build passed (PBS 937334).
Actual WITH_MAGMA=0 and auto builds also passed: each changed only the
adapter object and relinked, respectively removing/restoring libmagma in
DT_NEEDED. Auto consumes installed MAGMA without rebuilding it. Six
dry-run combinations verified fresh/existing installs and explicit targets.
After validation the tested snapshot was installed atomically as both
source/openmx and work/openmx, with SHA256:

OpenMX energy and forces
------------------------
The fixture starts from work/large_example/Pt63.dat, with private output
directories and absolute DFT_DATA19 path. It executes exactly three SCF
iterations with FP64 eigenvectors. Collinear spin-off, collinear spin-on,
and noncollinear spin with SOC are covered. The magnetic inputs start
with populations 8.5/7.5; noncollinear directions are 45/15 degrees.
This checks equivalent SCF iterations, not convergence of the physical
ground state. All 189 force components and finite energy components are
checked. Thresholds are 1e-7 Ha in total energy and 1e-7 Ha/bohr in force.

Together these are 23 completed OpenMX runs and 18 passing comparisons.

Reproduce with two binary snapshots inside a GPU allocation:
  python3 tests/run_mgpu_cluster_regression.py \
    --baseline /path/to/baseline/openmx --binary /path/to/new/openmx \
    --output /new/result/directory --ranks 2 --gpus 2
Use --prepare-only to inspect generated inputs before launching MPI.
The runner forces GPU thresholds because this fixture validates dispatch
and numerical behavior; these small shapes are not speed benchmarks.

Numerical kernels and failure handling
-------------------------------------
Actual CUDA tests cover real symmetric and complex Hermitian full and
leading-subset eigenvectors, upper/lower input, nonzero owner devices,
nondefault streams and repeated dispatch. Independent analytic spectra
and full residual/orthogonality oracles validate results. Two and three
physical GPUs were tested, including order 4096. MAGMA host-API tests also
cover interior subsets and eigenvalues-only modes. The paper fixtures
include clustered/geometric spectra and slab boundaries at 31/32/33 and
63/64/65. Its optional BC batch mode passed numerical and injected-failure
checks but regressed at order 1024; it remains disabled by default.

Custom GEMM passed all 64 numerical/transport cases on two and three
physical GPUs, maximum error 1.435e-14 against long-double reference sums.
Cases include real/complex N/T/C, alpha/beta, device scalars, padding,
rectangular outputs, nondefault streams, full-K tiling, peer/host transport
and rollback after a later worker failure. CPU arithmetic and failure
fixtures additionally cover four simulated devices.

Dependency-free CPU ownership/error tests passed 909,257 assertions for
the MAGMA adapter and 4,459 for runtime scope, topology, tuning cache,
cross-process admission, rollback and publication. These use UBSan and
fake CUDA/MAGMA interfaces; the physical-device results above separately
validate transport and arithmetic. The unavailable-MAGMA stub also passed.

Compute Sanitizer memcheck reported zero errors for two-GPU GEMM,
MAGMA and paper real/complex solves. Paper failure injection checks thread
startup, SBR, BC, backtransform and final GEMM; errors preserve caller
outputs and the following valid solve succeeds. The complex failure suite
also passed memcheck. Input-off, out-of-cluster and multi-node dispatch
gates have CPU and standalone hook coverage. The multi-node gate test
simulates the configuration; this is not a distributed ELPA/COSMA run.
The final build additionally passed actual NVLink automatic admission,
cluster-scope exit, one-GPU fallback, disabled/out-of-cluster/multi-node
gates and repeated complex n4096/2048 automatic solves. Complex n257/73
automatic selection, including all three trials and cached reuse, passed
memcheck with zero errors. Sanitizer timings are excluded from performance
claims because instrumentation changes the backend comparison.

Representative performance
--------------------------
Transfers, allocations and final publication are included. Forward GEMM
times include both products and use the retained actual GEMMul8 bridge
as the single-GPU baseline, including A100's accurate Ozaki policy.

Operation                 Single GPU       Two GPUs       Speedup
Real forward, n=8192       107.908 ms        91.614 ms      1.18x
Real forward, n=12288      331.253 ms       259.857 ms      1.27x
Complex forward, n=8192    472.908 ms       286.935 ms      1.65x

See mgpu_gemm.txt for rectangular and three-GPU measurements, resource
limits and conservative automatic thresholds. Adding a third GPU did not
improve the real square products versus these separate two-GPU runs.

Real full eigensolve n=4096: MAGMA's warmed trial was 1.835 s and paper's
4.041 s. Existing cuSOLVER was faster, so automatic selection keeps it.
The three-GPU complex n=4096, nev=2048 calibration measured 0.395 s for
cuSOLVER, 2.687 s for MAGMA and 5.016 s for paper. These are solver-call
measurements, not whole-SCF speedups. Auto times and caches each new shape;
its first-call calibration overhead is intentionally included in neither
steady-state claim nor a promise of faster overall SCF.
The final two-GPU complex n4096/2048 calibration measured 0.387 s for
cuSOLVER, 2.586 s for MAGMA and 4.707 s for paper. All validated; cuSOLVER
was selected. The full first call took 15.19 s with calibration, followed
by a cached call of 0.401 s.

Coverage limits
---------------
Physical GeForce, four-GPU, PCIe-only and multi-node application runs have
not been measured in this change. Existing GeForce defaults remain gated
away from automatic multi-GPU routing. The new paths retain the owner's
dense GPU storage and are not out-of-core. The paper implementation uses
its pipelined reduction/backtransform structure and adds complex support,
but does not reproduce all published low-level tuning or claim its reported
speedups. See paper_mgpu.txt for algorithm and tuning details.
